Key Concepts

The building blocks every HKDSE Economics student must master.

Scarcity

Resources are not enough to satisfy all wants. Relative, not absolute.

Opportunity Cost

The value of the highest-valued option forgone at the moment of decision.

Full Cost

Explicit cost plus implicit cost. The complete economic cost of a choice.

Interest

The price of earlier availability of resources. Exists wherever scarcity exists.

Allocation

Price competition vs non-price competition — who gets what, and why.

Demand & Supply

Plans at all prices, horizontal summation, and market equilibrium.
